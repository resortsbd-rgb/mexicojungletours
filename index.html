FAQ

Before you book.

Is transportation available?

Transportation is available for select hotels and pickup zones near Cancun, Playa del Carmen and Puerto Morelos. Confirm your pickup details before booking.

What should I bring?

Bring swimwear, a towel, closed-toe shoes, biodegradable sunscreen, insect repellent, a change of clothes and a waterproof phone pouch.

Are the cenotes natural?

Yes. The cenote experiences use natural freshwater cenotes along the Ruta de los Cenotes region.

Is it safe for families?

Family-friendly options are available. Age, height, weight and driver requirements vary by activity, so confirm details for your group before booking.

Can I book by WhatsApp?

Yes. Use the WhatsApp link to ask questions, confirm availability and request booking support.

What happens if it rains?

Light rain can be part of the jungle experience. If weather conditions affect safety, the team will help with rescheduling options.

What payment methods are accepted?

Online card payment is planned through the booking link. Additional payment methods should be confirmed by WhatsApp before arrival.
