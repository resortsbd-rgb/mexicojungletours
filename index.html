Family-connected land

The Kin-Ha Story

Kin-Ha is rooted in land connected to Francisco's family for generations. For more than 15 years, the area has welcomed travelers into a natural cenote environment along Puerto Morelos' Ruta de los Cenotes.

Think family-connected land, real Ruta de los Cenotes atmosphere and natural cenote access: one of the early local cenote experiences shaped by the place itself.

Natural differentiators

Why Kin-Ha feels different

Hidden cenote environment

Kin-Ha and Blanca Flor offer natural freshwater access surrounded by jungle and limestone formations.

Deep freshwater systems

Kin-Ha is known for impressive depth variations, with areas reaching approximately 30, 40 and up to 70 meters. Diving-related experiences are only for certified divers and require approval.

Platforms and performance energy

Blanca Flor is known as a place where experienced cliff jumpers and professionals practice from different platforms, including high platforms up to approximately 14 meters.

Natural movement and water

Kin-Ha's depth, water and atmosphere attract divers, athletes and performance artists looking for a unique natural setting.

Special access

Fátima: Limited Private Access

Fátima is a special cenote area not currently part of regular public tours. Access may be considered only for private groups, corporate groups or strategic partners, subject to approval and conservation guidelines.

It is known locally for a unique natural microorganism phenomenon, without guarantees or scientific claims on this page.

FAQ

Questions before booking

How long is the tour?

Timing depends on the selected route. The ATV Jungle Expedition is approximately 4 hours, before hotel transfer time.

Is transportation included?

Transportation is included with selected packages from Cancun, Playa del Carmen and Puerto Morelos pickup zones.

What should I bring?

Bring swimsuit, towel, comfortable clothes, suitable shoes, biodegradable sunscreen, bug spray and extra cash.

Are lockers available?

Lockers are available for belongings during the route.

Can children join?

Children can join selected experiences. Minors under 12 must be accompanied by an adult or parent.

What is child pricing?

Child pricing applies under 1.20m height.

Is lunch included?

Regional Mexican lunch or snacks are included depending on the selected package.

Are photos included?

Photos and videos are not included.

Is diving available?

Diving-related experiences are only for certified divers and require prior approval.

Can private groups book?

Private groups, corporate groups and strategic partners can ask about special access subject to approval and conservation guidelines.

What happens if it rains?

Light rain may be part of the jungle atmosphere. If safety is affected, the team will confirm available options.
