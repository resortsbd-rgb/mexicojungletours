FAQ

Clear answers before the trail.

Is transportation available?

Transportation is available for select hotel zones and meeting points near Cancun, Playa del Carmen and Puerto Morelos. Confirm pickup before payment.

What should I bring?

Bring swimwear, towel, closed-toe shoes, biodegradable sunscreen, insect repellent, a change of clothes and a waterproof phone pouch.

Are the cenotes natural?

Yes. The cenote route uses natural freshwater settings along the Puerto Morelos and Ruta de los Cenotes region.

Is it safe for families?

Family-friendly options are available. Activity requirements vary by age, height, weight and driving rules.

Can I book by WhatsApp?

Yes. Use WhatsApp to request availability, pickup details, activity fit and rate confirmation.

What happens if it rains?

Light rain can be part of the jungle atmosphere. If safety is affected, the team will help with available rescheduling options.

What payment methods are accepted?

Payment options are confirmed before booking. Online card payment can be connected once the live checkout account is ready.
